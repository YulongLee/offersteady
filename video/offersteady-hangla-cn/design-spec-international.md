# OfferSteady International Promo

## Goal
English, caption-led vertical promos for international interview candidates. The videos position OfferSteady as an AI interview copilot that turns scattered preparation material into a clear next step.

## Deliverables
- Short: 15 seconds, hook plus CTA, for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.
- Feature: 20 seconds, three product benefits plus CTA.
- Trust: 30 seconds, slower proof-led pacing for landing pages and YouTube.
- Each version is exported with BGM and without BGM.

## Visual direction
1080x1920, 30fps. Dark navy background (#07111f), mint accent (#63e6be), cyan secondary accent (#62d8ff), white text (#f4f8fb), muted text (#9fb0c2). Motion uses restrained spring reveals, rounded cards and a calm premium software feel.

## Storyboard
1. Hook: “Walk into your next interview prepared.”
2. Features: context from resume and job description; real-time help from voice, typed questions or screenshots; user remains in control.
3. CTA: “Prepare with OfferSteady” and `mianshiwen.cn`.

The international cut is caption-led because the repository has no approved English narration asset. Copy avoids unverified pricing, competitor comparisons and performance guarantees.
