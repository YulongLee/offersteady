## Context

OfferSteady currently has a durable domestic points/pass system, domestic payment providers, and a Chinese operator console. The Global deployment is isolated by server, database, domain, Web bundle, authentication method, and Compose project, but the Backend codebase is shared. Global Web currently exposes a read-only account view and keeps commerce disabled with `commerceProvider=none`.

The first international market is the US, UK, Australia, and Canada. The approved offer set is USD-denominated: Free ($0), Interview Day Pass ($9.99/24 hours), Pro Weekly ($49.99/7 days), Pro Monthly ($99.99/month), and Job Hunt ($199.99/90 days). Free is a one-time account grant. Interview Day Pass has 180 Copilot minutes, unlimited Screen Assist, and Resume/JD access. Pro offers are full-feature and marketed as Unlimited subject to a disclosed fair-use policy.

Creem is the selected future Merchant of Record. Its current public API creates checkout sessions from provider product IDs, supports one-time and recurring products, exposes customer portal links, and sends signed lifecycle events. Its documented webhook retry behavior makes provider event idempotency mandatory. Using the current planning assumption of 3.9% + $0.40 per successful transaction, estimated proceeds before tax adjustments, refunds, disputes, and OfferSteady AI cost are approximately $9.20, $47.64, $95.69, and $191.79 for the four paid offers. These figures are operational estimates, not persisted accounting truth.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Deliver a commercially credible, transparent Global pricing and purchase journey.
- Keep plan definitions and purchased benefit snapshots server-owned and auditable.
- Make checkout, fulfillment, renewal, cancellation, refund, and dispute handling secure and idempotent.
- Give operators Chinese-language controls and diagnostics without exposing secrets.
- Preserve active interviews during fair-use review and provider outages.
- Keep domestic commerce and all interview/audio/screenshot mechanics unchanged.

**Non-Goals:**

- Enabling Live Creem transactions without supplied credentials, provider products, webhook registration, legal copy, and explicit launch approval.
- Adding coupons, affiliates, teams, seats, regional price experiments, mobile in-app purchase, invoices generated by OfferSteady, or an internal card form.
- Claiming that Creem removes OfferSteady's own legal, privacy, refund-support, or consumer-disclosure obligations.
- Moving existing domestic balances, orders, users, or entitlements into Global.
- Persisting additional interview content for billing or fair-use decisions.

## Decisions

### Introduce an explicit server product-edition boundary

Backend configuration will add `OFFERSTEADY_PRODUCT_EDITION=cn|global`, defaulting to `cn`. Global commerce routes, repositories, workers, and admin views fail closed unless the edition is `global`; Global Compose sets it explicitly. The Chinese edition continues to use current domestic billing tables and providers.

This is preferred over hostname inference, which is fragile behind proxies, and over forking the Backend, which would increase security and feature drift.

### Use an independent Global commerce model

Global data will use dedicated plan versions, provider mappings, orders, provider events, subscriptions, entitlements, and usage counters. Paid entitlements snapshot the plan version, duration, quota, and feature flags at fulfillment time, so later catalogue edits cannot silently change an existing purchase.

This is preferred over extending domestic `billing_catalog_products`, whose CNY, duration, point, and provider constraints do not model USD subscriptions or plan-specific quotas. Migrations remain additive and harmless when present in a Chinese database because Chinese runtime paths never access these tables.

### Publish five fixed commercial offer codes with versioned benefits

The initial offer codes and billing modes are:

| Code | Price | Billing | Entitlement |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `global-free` | $0 | one-time account grant | 15 Copilot minutes, 3 Screen Assist uses, no expiry until consumed |
| `global-interview-pass` | $9.99 | one-time | 24 hours, 180 Copilot minutes, unlimited Screen Assist, Resume/JD |
| `global-pro-weekly` | $49.99 | one-time | 7 days, Unlimited full product |
| `global-pro-monthly` | $99.99 | recurring monthly | provider billing period, Unlimited full product |
| `global-job-hunt` | $199.99 | one-time | 90 days, Unlimited full product |

The two weekly offers and Job Hunt are one-time purchases to avoid surprise renewals. Pro Monthly clearly discloses automatic renewal and cancellation before checkout. Global pages show USD base prices and state that Creem presents the final local currency and applicable tax at checkout.

Operators may edit presentation, publication, display order, featured state, expected USD price, benefits, and provider mappings through versioned drafts. Activating a changed benefit set creates a new immutable plan version. Offer codes and the distinction between one-time and recurring are not silently mutated while provider orders exist.

### Grant Free exactly once without requiring a card

An authenticated Global account receives the Free grant at verified email registration. Existing Global accounts receive it lazily on their first entitlement read. A database uniqueness constraint on `(user_id, grant_kind)` guarantees one grant per account. Exhausted Free usage never resets, and deletion/re-registration protections rely on the durable authenticated account identity rather than browser storage.

### Model quota and Unlimited separately

Copilot minutes and Screen Assist use nullable limits: a positive integer means metered, while `NULL` plus the matching entitlement flag means Unlimited. Resume/JD and knowledge-base access are explicit feature flags. Interview Day Pass includes Resume/JD but not the broader Pro knowledge-base feature; all Pro offers include the complete current Global product.

Usage reservation and settlement remain atomic and idempotent. Metered Copilot reserves time in bounded increments and reconciles actual elapsed usage. Screen Assist reserves one use before model invocation and releases it on an internal failure. Provider or billing failure must not erase a valid active entitlement.

### Make fair use transparent and session-safe

Unlimited means no ordinary minute or Screen Assist balance. It remains subject to public, concise restrictions against account sharing, automated extraction, resale, excessive parallel sessions, and abusive machine-generated traffic. Technical protection includes one active live interview per account, bounded request concurrency, existing API rate limits, and anomaly review based only on usage metadata.

Automated fair-use detection SHALL NOT terminate or degrade an interview already in progress. It may block creation of a later session only after a durable decision with reason, expiry/review state, and support path. Ordinary users never see hidden minute/session caps. This avoids the trust failure common when a product advertises Unlimited but silently stops during a long interview.

### Keep Creem behind a replaceable server adapter

The Backend provider port covers checkout creation/retrieval, product validation, customer portal creation, subscription retrieval/cancellation, refunds, and reconciliation. The adapter uses Test or Live endpoints selected server-side. Browser code sends only an internal offer code and idempotency key; it never sends an authoritative price or provider product ID.

Checkout creation persists an internal pending order before calling Creem, passes an opaque internal reference through `request_id`/metadata, and stores only the returned checkout URL and provider object identifiers. API keys and webhook secrets remain in environment/KMS-backed server configuration and never enter `VITE_` variables, JSON responses, database plaintext, admin forms, or logs.

The adapter normalizes Creem's current wire contract at the provider boundary: product and subscription retrieval use query parameters, product prices are integer minor units, and webhook checkout/order/product/subscription objects may be nested. Creem environment labels (`test`, `sandbox`, `local`, `prod`, and `production`) map to OfferSteady's isolated `test|live` modes before business validation. Subscription period fields accept Creem's ISO `current_period_*_date` form while retaining compatibility with previously observed numeric aliases.

Checkout POST is not automatically replayed after an ambiguous transport failure because Creem documents `request_id` as a reference but not as a provider-side idempotency guarantee. OfferSteady's internal order remains idempotent and recoverable through reconciliation. Read operations may retry within the bounded provider policy. A failed webhook delivery may be reclaimed only when the provider event ID and exact payload hash both match; entitlement source uniqueness keeps an interrupted retry from granting twice.

### Fulfill only from verified provider truth

The webhook endpoint reads the exact raw body, verifies `creem-signature` using HMAC-SHA256 and constant-time comparison, validates environment, product mapping, internal reference, customer, currency, amount, and provider status, then stores the provider event ID under a unique constraint. Entitlement/order/subscription changes and the processed-event marker commit in one database transaction.

`checkout.completed` fulfills one-time purchases. `subscription.paid` fulfills or renews a subscription period; `subscription.active` synchronizes state but is not a second grant. Cancellation at period end keeps access to the confirmed period end. Paused/expired states prevent future access after the confirmed effective time. Refund and dispute events record reversals and invoke the approved entitlement policy without creating negative usage balances. Duplicate or reordered events converge on the same state.

The browser return page is informational and polls the internal order. Redirect parameters and a successful-looking page never grant access.

### Use layered activation gates

Commerce readiness requires all of the following in the Global edition: provider mode selected, Test/Live API key present, webhook secret present, webhook URL configured, active provider products mapped, provider products matching expected amount/currency/billing mode, signed webhook acceptance tested, legal/refund links configured, and an explicit operator activation. Test and Live credentials, mappings, events, and activation flags are isolated.

Saving configuration automatically disables new checkout creation until validation passes again. Historical webhook processing and order reconciliation remain enabled while new checkout creation is disabled.

### Add Chinese operator controls without secret disclosure

The existing Chinese admin gains a Global commerce area visible only on the Global deployment. It contains plan drafts/versions, Creem mapping and readiness, masked secret status, Test/Live activation, webhook diagnostics, order/subscription/refund/dispute search, reconciliation actions, estimated provider fee/net revenue, usage cost, and gross-margin alerts.

Secret values are injected outside the browser. Admin can rotate by replacing server secret references, but reads return only configured/not-configured and fingerprints. Sensitive actions require `payments.manage`, recent MFA, confirmation, reason, rate limiting, and immutable audit events.

The Global console also has a dedicated member-support workspace. Search is explicit, bounded, and paginated across Global email login, display name, and user ID; it never polls in the background. Member detail composes identity metadata with Global entitlements, remaining usage, orders, and subscriptions. A manual grant snapshots one current paid plan into an `admin`-sourced entitlement and never fabricates a Creem order or recurring subscription; revocation targets one entitlement and preserves financial history. Both actions require `payments.manage`, recent MFA, confirmation, reason, idempotency, and audit.

### Protect unit economics without weakening the product

Commercial monitoring calculates revenue from provider-confirmed order currency/amount, records provider fee data when available, and estimates AI/ASR/storage cost from existing usage metadata without storing content. Alerts trigger when estimated variable cost exceeds a configurable share of net revenue, refund/dispute rates rise, or Unlimited usage becomes anomalous. These alerts do not alter an active interview automatically.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Unlimited Screen Assist can create negative unit economics] → Measure per-entitlement model cost, disclose fair use, rate-limit automation, and review anomalies without hidden ordinary-user caps.
- [Weekly recurring billing would increase chargebacks and distrust] → Launch weekly and 90-day offers as one-time purchases; only Monthly auto-renews with explicit disclosure.
- [Provider events can duplicate, arrive late, or reorder] → Persist unique event IDs and compare provider effective periods/state inside serializable fulfillment transactions.
- [Admin price and Creem product can drift] → Validate provider amount, currency, and billing mode before activation and fail checkout closed on mismatch.
- [Refund or dispute after usage creates loss] → Preserve immutable accounting, revoke only remaining entitlement according to policy, flag abuse, and never fabricate a negative usage balance.
- [Merchant-of-Record coverage may change] → Verify the signed Creem merchant agreement and live account coverage before launch; treat public tax/fee claims as current operational inputs, not contractual guarantees.
- [Shared Backend code could leak Global behavior into China] → Default to the Chinese edition, use separate routes/tables/configuration, and run domestic regression and no-restart checks.
- [A hard billing dependency could interrupt interviews] → Cache only already-authorized entitlement state for a bounded interval and fail open for an active session, while failing closed for new checkout and new-session authorization.

## Migration Plan

1. Record Global Backend/Web/Admin/database release and rollback points; verify Chinese production health.
2. Ship additive tables, edition configuration, dormant provider adapter, and admin read-only views with commerce disabled.
3. Backfill one-time Free grants for existing Global accounts idempotently and verify quota behavior with synthetic users.
4. Configure Creem Test credentials and five Test products; validate mappings and register the Test webhook.
5. Complete synthetic checkout, duplicate/reordered webhook, renewal, cancellation, refund, dispute, reconciliation, and customer-portal tests.
6. Ship Global pricing UI while checkout remains disabled, then complete responsive and accessibility review.
7. Configure Live credentials/products/webhook, legal/refund links, and run a real low-value acceptance purchase and refund.
8. After explicit approval, enable new Live checkout creation. Monitor conversion, payment success, webhook lag, refunds, disputes, provider fees, AI cost, and margin.
9. Rollback disables new checkout first, restores Web/Admin/Backend images if needed, and leaves provider event ingestion/reconciliation available. Additive billing records and migrations are not deleted.

## Open Questions

- Creem Test and Live API keys, webhook secrets, and product IDs will be supplied later and must not be pasted into source control or chat output.
- Final Terms, Privacy, Fair Use, auto-renewal disclosure, refund policy, support SLA, and consumer-law review require business approval before Live activation.
- Final margin alert thresholds depend on measured ASR/LLM/vision cost from test and early production traffic.
