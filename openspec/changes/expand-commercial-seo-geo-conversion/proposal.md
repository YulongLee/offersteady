## Why

The public site now has a strong crawlability and GEO baseline, but its indexable footprint still concentrates on eight short capability pages and does not answer several commercial decision, installation, trust, and platform-setup intents. A focused second phase can turn the existing technical foundation into qualified discovery and conversion paths without changing the authenticated interview product.

## What Changes

- Publish truthful, server-delivered public pages for pricing logic, desktop downloads, security and data boundaries, product identity, and support contact paths.
- Publish a small high-intent guide cluster for macOS permissions, Feishu audio setup, Tencent Meeting audio setup, and structured STAR interview answers.
- Deepen guide authority with visible ownership, reviewed/updated dates, source notes, direct-answer passages, and Article-compatible JSON-LD without inventing authors, legal entities, customers, outcomes, or statistics.
- Connect every new page through crawlable navigation, sitemap membership, route-specific metadata, self-canonicals, and explicit Nginx mappings.
- Make AI crawler intent explicit for search/discovery agents, shorten overlong homepage social titles, and extend `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, and `public-facts.json` with the new canonical sources.
- Extend deterministic release checks and public route smoke tests while preserving all authenticated application, interview, realtime, material, payment, membership, admin, and desktop behavior.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `public-commercial-decision-pages`: Crawlable public pricing, download, security, identity, and contact pages with truthful calls to action and no dependency on authenticated state.
- `public-search-content-authority`: Curated high-intent guide pages with substantive unique content, visible maintenance metadata, source boundaries, internal links, and appropriate Article/WebPage structured data.
- `public-geo-entity-trust`: Consistent public product facts, explicit search-oriented AI crawler access, canonical source discovery, and release validation without unverified entity or performance claims.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Web public assets: new static HTML entries, shared public-search styles, homepage and guide navigation, metadata, sitemap, robots, GEO discovery files, and regression tests.
- Ingress: explicit allowlisted public routes and unchanged cache/index controls for product and sensitive routes.
- Documentation: SEO page map, monitoring notes, audit/action-plan evidence, and an execution log update.
- No backend API, database, authentication, interview, realtime audio, screenshot, material, billing, payment, membership, admin, or desktop protocol changes.
