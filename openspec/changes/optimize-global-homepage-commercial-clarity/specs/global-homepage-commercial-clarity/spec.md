## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Concise commercial homepage journey
The homepage SHALL present a short AI interview guidance headline, a concise benefit description, a clear Start free action, existing verified downloads, four distinct feature benefits, canonical pricing earlier than the optional comparison, FAQ and a closing free action. It SHALL retain a concise responsible-use note and visible operator/support/policy links.

#### Scenario: Visitor evaluates the service
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the English homepage
- **THEN** the page explains the service with a headline of no more than 10 words and a main description of no more than 30 words
- **AND** Start free links to existing login without opening checkout
- **AND** current prices, included benefits, billing distinctions, access start and payment availability remain available

### Requirement: Honest proof and accessible supporting content
The homepage SHALL preserve existing feedback provenance and video assets, disclose Free limitations accurately, and keep the dated competitor comparison available through a keyboard-operable disclosure. It SHALL NOT add fabricated proof, unsupported performance or outcome claims, or new tracking.

#### Scenario: Visitor seeks more detail
- **WHEN** a visitor expands comparison or FAQ, or plays either video
- **THEN** the requested content is available with native accessible controls
- **AND** both videos remain muted initially, user controlled, inline, metadata-preloaded and not autoplaying
- **AND** existing quotes and comparison data are unchanged

### Requirement: Responsive static parity and isolation
The homepage SHALL expose equivalent commercial claims in direct HTML and React, preserve unique metadata/canonical and independent legal routes, and remain usable at desktop and narrow mobile widths without new runtime requests.

#### Scenario: No JavaScript or narrow viewport
- **WHEN** the built homepage is requested without JavaScript or viewed at 320, 390, 900 or 1440 pixels
- **THEN** benefits, current pricing, FAQs, support and operator are readable, and interactive presentation has no unintended page overflow
- **AND** no China, authentication, payment, entitlement, AI or interview logic changes are required
