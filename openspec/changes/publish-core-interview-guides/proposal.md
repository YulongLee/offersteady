## Why

The new guidance hub has useful setup content but does not yet answer four high-frequency preparation intents: self-introduction, project-experience explanation, technical-interview preparation, and common interview questions. Publishing a reviewed first cluster gives search visitors complete answers and a truthful path into the existing preparation workflow.

## What Changes

- Publish substantive public guides at `/guides/self-introduction`, `/guides/project-experience`, `/guides/technical-interview`, and `/guides/common-interview-questions`.
- Give each article a distinct search intent, direct-answer passages, actionable templates/checklists, limitations, visible ownership and dates, Article/Breadcrumb structured data, and relevant internal links.
- Link the four guides from `/guides`, `/interview-questions`, homepage/guide discovery surfaces where appropriate, sitemap, and GEO sources.
- Extend deterministic release checks and route mappings from 20 to 24 public canonical pages.
- Preserve all authenticated product behavior and avoid fabricated questions, companies, candidates, outcomes, statistics, or personal experiences.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `public-core-interview-guides`: Search visitors can learn four foundational interview-preparation methods from complete, reviewable, privacy-safe public articles.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Four static public guide documents plus changes to hubs, navigation/discovery files, source/build checks, Nginx routing/CSP, tests, and content documentation.
- No backend, database, authentication, interview, audio, screenshot, material, billing, admin, or desktop changes.
