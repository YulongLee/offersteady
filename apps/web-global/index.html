A few things worth knowing.

What can I try for free?

Free includes a one-time allowance of 15 Copilot minutes and 3 Screen Assist uses. No card is required. Resume/JD context, knowledge materials and Written Exam mode require an eligible paid plan.

See plan details
How does guidance use my experience?

You choose the resume, job description and optional knowledge materials for each session. These help make suggestions relevant to you. Check every suggestion against your actual experience; AI can still make mistakes.

Explore the features
Does it work on my computer?

The desktop Companion is available for Windows and macOS. Use it on the computer providing audio or screenshots, with the Web workspace in your browser. Compatibility depends on your operating system, audio route and permissions.

Check setup details
Do the paid plans renew automatically?

Only Pro Monthly renews automatically until cancelled. Interview Day Pass, Pro Weekly and Job Hunt are one-time purchases. Paid access starts at confirmed payment, not first use.

Review billing and access
Why can't I purchase a paid plan yet?

Online checkout requires payment provider merchant approval and completed production configuration. Paid purchase controls remain disabled. Free access is available without a card.

Contact support
Can I use AI assistance in every interview?

No. Follow the interview organiser's rules for AI assistance, recording, screen capture and confidentiality. Obtain consent where required. If a feature is not permitted, do not use it in that session.

Read the usage rules
What happens to my interview data?

Raw audio is not stored by default. Uploaded materials, transcripts, screenshots and generated results are processed for the features you use and may be retained under the service's retention and deletion controls.
